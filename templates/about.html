About The Dojo Bay

The purpose of DojoBay is to provide access to those who do not have access to a dojo. We encourage users to operate their own nodes rather than relying on third parties. We do not collect any information about the users who access these nodes. However, since we are a federation of individuals located in different jurisdictions, we assume that everyone is acting honestly.

We cannot control when a dojo is down, as only the owner of the node has the capability to restart it. We will make an effort to keep the directory updated with only running dojos. Please conduct your own due diligence.

We are not affiliated with Samourai or Ashigaru no Ronin Dojo, but we appreciate their efforts and contributions to the community.

#FreeSamourai

Contact

If you would like to have your dojo included in the directory, please send us an email (please note that our reply could fall under the spam folder) with the details, and we will be happy to include it. The main purpose of the site is to provide dojos to those who need them.

Thank you in advance for your support.
